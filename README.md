# 📊 Advanced Business Performance & Profit Optimization Dashboard | Business Analyst Case Study
## Business Analyst Case Study

This project is an end-to-end Business Analyst case study developed in Microsoft Excel using the Sample Superstore dataset.

The objective of this project is to transform raw sales transaction data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, business analysis, KPI development, interactive dashboards, and business recommendations.

The dashboard enables decision-makers to monitor key business metrics, evaluate performance across different regions and product categories, identify sales trends, and support data-driven business decisions.

---

## Business Problem

The company generates sales across multiple regions, product categories, and customer segments. However, the sales data exists as raw transaction records, making it difficult for management to understand overall business performance.

Without a centralized reporting dashboard, decision-makers spend significant time preparing reports instead of analyzing business performance. This makes it difficult to identify profitable products, monitor KPIs, compare regional performance, and make timely business decisions.

To solve this problem, an interactive Business Performance & Profit Optimization Dashboard was developed in Microsoft Excel to transform raw business data into clear, actionable insights.

---

# Dataset

- **Dataset Name:** Sample Superstore Dataset
- **Dataset Type:** Retail Sales Transaction Data
- **Records:** Approximately 9,994 sales transactions
- **Purpose:** Business Performance Analysis and Profit Optimization
- **Fields Included:** Order Date, Region, Segment, Category, Sub-Category, Product Name, Sales, Profit, Quantity, Discount, Customer Information, Shipping Details, and more.

# Business Questions

This project was developed to answer the following business questions:

1. What are the company's total Sales, Profit, Orders, Quantity Sold, and Profit Margin?
2. How have sales changed over time?
3. Which region generates the highest sales and profit?
4. Which product category contributes the most to sales?
5. Which products generate the highest sales?
6. Which regions or categories require business improvement?
7. What business insights can be identified from the data?
8. What recommendations can improve overall business performance and profitability?

---

#  Key Business Insights

- The company generated strong overall sales, but the profit margin indicates opportunities to improve profitability.
- Sales performance varies across different time periods, suggesting seasonal demand patterns.
- Regional performance is not uniform, highlighting opportunities to improve lower-performing regions.
- Product categories contribute differently to total sales and profit.
- The Top 10 Products generate a significant share of overall sales and should be prioritized in business planning.

  ---

#  Business Recommendations

- Review pricing and discount strategies to improve profit margins.
- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing products and categories.
- Develop improvement plans for lower-performing regions.
- Monitor KPIs regularly to support faster business decisions.
- Use dashboard insights to improve inventory planning and business performance.
